Who we are

One nation.
One passion.

Vespa Club Malaysia was founded to unite Vespa enthusiasts from across the nation under one community. Beyond riding, we strengthen connections among local Vespa communities, encourage collaboration between clubs and preserve the heritage and spirit of Vespa for future generations.

Vespa is more than just a scooter. It is a lifestyle, a friendship and a bond that connects people from all walks of life.

Mission

Vision
