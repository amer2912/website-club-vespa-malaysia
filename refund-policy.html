Refund Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Privacy Policy · Refund Policy · Shipping & Returns

Ringkasan (BM): Yuran keahlian dan tiket acara tidak dikembalikan selepas dibayar, kecuali bayaran berganda atau tersilap, acara dibatalkan, atau keahlian/tiket tidak dapat diberikan. Mohon melalui emel dalam masa 30 hari; bayaran balik diproses dalam 14 hari bekerja selepas diluluskan.

This policy applies to payments made on vespaclubmalaysia.com for membership fees and for event tickets or packs sold by Vespa Club Malaysia.

1. Membership fees

  • The new member fee and the renewal fee are non-refundable once your membership has been activated.
  • If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, we refund the extra amount in full after checking the payment.
  • If money was deducted but your membership was not activated, contact us with your payment reference. We will either activate your membership or refund you in full.

2. Event tickets and packs sold by VCM

  • Tickets and packs are non-refundable once paid, including when you cannot attend. You may ask for your pack to be posted after the event (see our Shipping & Returns policy).
  • If the event is cancelled by VCM, you receive a full refund.
  • If the event is postponed, your ticket stays valid for the new date. If you cannot attend the new date, you may ask for a refund within 14 days of the announcement.
  • If an item is sold out after you paid and we cannot supply it, you receive a full refund for that item.
  • T-shirt size exchanges are made at the event counter, subject to stock.

3. Events run by other organisers

Some events on our website are run by other organisers, and payment goes to that organiser. Their refund terms apply. We will help you contact the organiser.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email us within 30 days of payment with your name, member number or order number (for example VD-0123), payment reference and the reason.
  2. We reply within 5 working days.
  3. Approved refunds are paid within 14 working days to your original payment method or by bank transfer.
