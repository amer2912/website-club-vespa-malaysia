Our leadership

Leading a new era

President

Mohd Fyrdous Hamdan

Currently serving in government administration, Fyrdous brings his professional experience and leadership perspective to guide Vespa Club Malaysia towards a new era of growth, stronger national engagement, and greater value for its members.

AH
Vice President

Haji Abd Halim

A retired Royal Malaysian Navy (TLDM) serviceman, Haji Abd Halim brings his years of service, discipline, and experience to support the club's mission of strengthening unity, fostering camaraderie, and developing a stronger national Vespa community.

Together, the leadership team is committed to building a more connected VCM, delivering meaningful membership benefits, and establishing national events that celebrate Malaysia's Vespa culture.
