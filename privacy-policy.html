Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · Privacy Policy · Refund Policy · Shipping & Returns

Ringkasan (BM): Kami mengumpul data peribadi ahli dan pembeli tiket hanya untuk urusan keahlian, bayaran dan acara kelab, mengikut Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (PDPA). Data tidak dijual. Ahli boleh menyemak dan membetulkan profil sendiri di portal ahli, atau menghubungi kami.

Kelab Vespa Malaysia (Vespa Club Malaysia) ("VCM", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through vespaclubmalaysia.com, the member portal and our event ticket pages, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Personal data we collect

  • Members: full name, NRIC or passport number, nationality, gender, age, email, mobile number, address, state, beneficiary name, NRIC and relationship, OKU registration number (if given), Vespa model and plate number, T-shirt size, club or group, Vespa World Club number (if any), and your membership and payment records.
  • Event buyers: name, mobile number, email, Vespa model (optional), T-shirt size, survey answers (if any), and your order, payment and collection records.
  • Payments: we keep the amount, date and payment reference. Card numbers and online banking log-in details are entered on the payment provider's page and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Website use: privacy-friendly, cookie-free visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that do not identify you.

2. Why we use it

  • To keep the club's membership register, as required by our constitution and the Registrar of Societies, and to issue your member number and digital member card.
  • To process membership fees and event purchases, and to issue receipts, tickets and QR codes.
  • To run events, including pack and T-shirt collection, lucky draws and attendance.
  • To contact you about your membership, payments, events and club matters.
  • Beneficiary details are used only for the club welfare purposes for which you provided them.

3. Who we share it with

  • Authorised committee members (AJK) who need the data to manage membership and events. Access to identity numbers, addresses and beneficiary details is limited to authorised officers.
  • Event organisers: when you buy a ticket for an event run by another organiser on our website, your order details are shared with that organiser for that event.
  • Service providers that run the website for us: hosting (Netlify), database and log-in (Supabase), payment gateways (ToyyibPay, Billplz) and email delivery. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in a secured database. Each user can only see their own records, and committee access is controlled by role. We review access regularly.

5. How long we keep it

We keep membership data while you are a member and for as long as our record-keeping duties as a registered society require. Event order data is kept for as long as needed for the event, accounts and audit.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and correct most of your details yourself in the member portal.
  • You may ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer keep your membership active.

7. Cookies and storage

The member portal keeps your log-in session in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
