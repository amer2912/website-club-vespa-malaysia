Shipping & Returns

Effective 27 November 2026 · Privacy Policy · Refund Policy · Shipping & Returns

Ringkasan (BM): Pek dan baju acara diambil di tapak acara dengan kod QR. Pembeli yang tidak dapat hadir boleh memohon pos selepas acara dengan kos pos ditanggung pembeli (alamat di Malaysia sahaja). Barang rosak boleh ditukar dalam masa 7 hari selepas diterima.

1. Event packs and T-shirts

  • Event packs and T-shirts are collected at the event venue by showing your QR ticket at the counter. They are not posted by default.
  • If you cannot attend, you may ask for your pack to be posted after the event. Email or message us with your order number, recipient name, full address and phone number before the date we announce for the event.
  • Postage is paid by the buyer. We tell you the cost before posting (it depends on whether the address is in Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah or Sarawak).
  • Posted items are sent within 7 working days after the event, to addresses in Malaysia only, and we send you the tracking number.
  • Items that only work at the venue (for example food vouchers) cannot be used after the event.

2. Membership

Membership is delivered digitally: your member number and digital member card appear in the member portal once payment is confirmed. The new member T-shirt is handed over at club events or by arrangement with the committee; if you ask for it to be posted, postage is paid by the member.

3. Merchandise

When merchandise is sold on our website, the delivery method, postage and estimated delivery time are shown before you pay.

4. Returns and exchanges

  • Wrong size: exchange at the event counter, subject to stock.
  • Damaged or faulty item: tell us within 7 days of receiving it, with a photo. We replace the item, or refund it if no replacement is available.
  • Items must be unused and unwashed for an exchange, unless faulty.
